## Who We Are

**IA Freelancers** is an independent team of three freelance engineers specialized in **Artificial Intelligence** and **Data Science**.

We partner with companies, startups and agencies to design, build and deploy AI solutions that solve real business problems — from raw data exploration to fully integrated systems running in production.

Our expertise covers:

`Data Science` · `Machine Learning` · `Deep Learning` · `Artificial Intelligence` · `Generative AI` · `LLMs` · `NLP` · `Computer Vision` · `RAG` · `AI Agents`

We work end-to-end: **understanding the business need → exploiting the data → developing the solution → deploying and integrating it into your environment.**

> We are not selling models. We are delivering solutions that create measurable value.

## Services

### 1. Data Science & Data Analytics
Making sense of your data before building anything on top of it.

- Data exploration, cleaning and preparation (EDA)
- Statistical analysis and hypothesis testing
- Feature engineering and data pipelines
- Interactive dashboards and business reporting
- KPI definition and data-driven decision support

### 2. Machine Learning & Predictive Analytics
Predicting outcomes to anticipate rather than react.

- Classification, regression and clustering models
- Demand, sales and churn forecasting
- Recommendation engines
- Anomaly and fraud detection
- Time series analysis and forecasting
- Model evaluation, tuning and interpretability

### 3. Deep Learning
Advanced neural architectures for complex, high-dimensional problems.

- Custom neural network design and training
- Transfer learning and fine-tuning of pretrained models
- CNNs, RNNs, LSTMs and Transformer architectures
- Model optimization for accuracy, latency and cost

### 4. Computer Vision
Giving software the ability to understand images and video.

- Image classification and object detection
- Image segmentation and OCR / document understanding
- Quality control and visual inspection
- Video analysis and real-time detection pipelines

### 5. NLP — Natural Language Processing
Extracting meaning and structure from text.

- Text classification and sentiment analysis
- Named entity recognition and information extraction
- Automatic summarization and translation
- Semantic search and document similarity
- Multilingual text processing

### 6. Generative AI & LLMs
Leveraging large language models for business use cases.

- LLM-powered applications and assistants
- Prompt engineering and optimization
- Fine-tuning and domain adaptation
- Structured generation and content automation
- Model selection, benchmarking and cost/performance arbitration

### 7. RAG — Retrieval-Augmented Generation
Connecting LLMs to your own knowledge base.

- Private document assistants and internal knowledge bots
- Document ingestion, chunking and embedding pipelines
- Vector database design and indexing
- Hybrid and semantic search strategies
- Grounded answers with source citation and hallucination control

### 8. AI Agents & Intelligent Automation
Autonomous systems that reason, decide and act.

- Task-oriented and multi-step AI agents
- Tool calling and external API orchestration
- Multi-agent workflows
- Business process automation
- Human-in-the-loop control and validation

### 9. AI Integration, Deployment & Monitoring
Making sure the solution actually runs — reliably.

- REST API development and model serving
- Containerization and cloud deployment
- Integration into existing systems and applications
- CI/CD for machine learning workflows
- Performance monitoring, drift detection and maintenance

## Our Approach

A structured, transparent process — from the first conversation to a solution running in production.

| # | Step | What happens |
|:--:|:--|:--|
| **1** | **Understanding** | We start with your business need, not with technology. We clarify the objective, the constraints and how success will be measured. |
| **2** | **Data** | We explore, assess and prepare the available data — quality, volume, structure and feasibility. |
| **3** | **Design** | We define the right technical approach: architecture, models, tools and expected performance. |
| **4** | **Development** | We build, train and iterate — with rigorous evaluation at each step. |
| **5** | **Integration** | We connect the solution to your existing systems, tools and workflows. |
| **6** | **Deployment** | We ship to production with reliable, scalable and maintainable infrastructure. |
| **7** | **Improvement** | We monitor performance, detect drift and continuously improve the solution over time. |
